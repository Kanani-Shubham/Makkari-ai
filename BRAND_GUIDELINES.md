# Makkari AI — Production Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Concept & Identity
Makkari is an **Intelligence Operating System** designed to move at the speed of thought. The visual identity represents high intelligence, calm productivity, precision, and warm elegance.

### 🎨 Color Palette
- **Terracotta Primary (`#D97757`)**: Main brand focal color representing energy and warmth.
- **Warm Bronze Secondary (`#A86A4A`)**: Accent ribbon stroke in emblem.
- **Warm Canvas Background (`#F7F6F3`)**: Light mode body background.
- **Oled Obsidian (`#111111` / `#121212`)**: Dark mode body background.
- **Deep Slate Charcoal (`#1A1A1A`)**: Primary text color in light theme.
- **Soft Muted Slate (`#6B6B6B`)**: Secondary text & caption color.
- **Clean Border (`#E8E5E0` / `#2E2E2E`)**: Neutral card & input border.

### 📐 Logo System & Geometry
- **Primary Emblem**: Geometric folded 'M' ribbon combined with an 8-ray intelligence spark mark.
- **Typography**: Wordmark uses `Newsreader` (Serif Display) + `Inter` (Sans Tech).
- **Clearance**: Minimum clear space equal to 50% of the emblem's height (`0.5H`).
